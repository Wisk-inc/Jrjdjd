Meet Automa: an AI agents app, made in Jamaica.

Automa 1.9.9 is an Android app that runs a whole company of AI agents for you. It works on free AI models with no key, no account and no card — including Moan, a Jamaican model that answers in Patois — and the server can be the phone in your hand. You can download it now.

The short version

Automa is an Android app, made in Jamaica by CorX Labs, that runs a company of AI agents from a phone. You create an organization with a goal, hire agents into an org chart, give them work in plain words, and approve the things that matter — hires, budgets, plans. Every task has one owner, a plan, a history and a cost.

Version 1.9.9, for Android 8.0 or newer, is a free 23.9 MB APK download. It runs on twenty-plus free chat models and two free image models, or on your own Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek and Grok keys.

Why a phone

Most AI tools assume three things: a US-dollar credit card, a desktop computer, and an English that is not the one spoken at home in Jamaica. Automa drops all three. The free models need no card. The Automa server — where the agents actually do their work — can run on the phone itself, inside the free Termux app. And Moan talks Patois.

The other bet is about what a phone is good for. It is a poor place to watch work happen and a good place to unblock it. So the home screen leads with Needs you: the inbox, approvals, the files an agent is waiting on, and who is working on what. Everything an agent can finish on its own stays off that screen.

What it does

  • Chats with agents that stream their replies and their thinking, search the web with sources, read attachments and draw pictures.
  • Tasks and approvals: agents plan the work, ask you questions inside the task, and wait for your yes before they hire or spend.
  • Real files: Excel spreadsheets with formulas, PowerPoint decks with charts and Word documents.
  • App Studio: build, run and share apps in 69 languages with an AI pair or a whole AI team.
  • Terminals for Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI on the device, and App Inspector to take an Android app apart.
  • Costs by agent, task and month, with hard budget stops.

Free models, and Moan

Pick a free model and the agent runs on it, with no key, account or card. The list covers Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Gemma 4, Qwen 3.8 27B and more, plus SDXL Lightning and SDXL Base for pictures. Every model says how it is doing — ready, busy, at today’s limit, or closed — and the Que engine underneath spreads requests so many people asking at once all get answers.

Moan is Automa’s own Jamaican model. It answers in Patois first and in Standard Jamaican English for formal documents and code, and while it is in use Automa keeps reading Jamaican sources — the Gleaner, the Observer, JIS, the Office of the Prime Minister, Parliament, the Bank of Jamaica and the Laws of Jamaica — into a local index, so its replies come with source, date and link. Asked in Patois how to register a small bakery in Kingston, it answered in Patois.

Five weeks, 1.0 to 1.9.7

Automa 1.0.0 is dated 27 September 2026. In the five weeks since, more than twenty releases added the org chart on phones, running the server on the phone itself, App Studio, Terminals and App Inspector, the free models, Moan, the Que engine, notifications, and a full audit release that fixed every confirmed security finding. 1.9.7 made it steadier: coming back to the app now keeps you on your page, and deleting a chat mid-reply stops the reply at once. 1.9.8 followed on 8 November with fixes under the hood: Automa on the phone starts even when an old queued run can’t be verified, and web search falls back to Bing when DuckDuckGo refuses. 1.9.9 came the next day, after a scan of every page: when the free AI service asks Automa to slow down, it now waits and carries on instead of failing the reply.

The full history, release by release, is on the Automa page and inside the app under About Automa.

What it is not

Automa is Android only, and its AI needs the internet. There is no iPhone version. With the server on the phone, Automa itself keeps working offline, but the models do not. Free models have daily limits and a shared monthly allowance, and Moan’s built-in index covers Jamaican sources — for the rest of the Caribbean, agents research the web instead.

How to get it

Automa 1.9.9 is a signed APK, and downloading it takes one tap — 23.9 MB, for Android 8.0 or newer. It has also been submitted to APKPure, and will be released there once APKPure finishes its review. Every release since 1.8.2 is signed with the same key and installs over the last one, keeping your data. The install steps and every screen in the app are on the Automa page.

FAQ

Is there a Jamaican AI app?

Yes. Automa is an Android app made in Jamaica by CorX Labs that runs a company of AI agents from a phone. Its free models include Moan, a Jamaican model that answers in Patois and knows Jamaica’s news, law and government.

Is Automa Jamaica's first AI app?

Automa is one of the first AI agent apps built in Jamaica, made by the 14-year-old founder of CorX Labs — the lab behind CorX3.8-27B, Jamaica’s first large open-weight language model. Other Jamaican AI platforms launched in 2026 too, so CorX Labs does not claim it was the very first.

Is Automa free to use?

You can run it without paying for AI: twenty-plus free chat models and two free image models need no key, no account and no card. Paid models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini run on your own keys, and every token is counted against a budget you set.

Does Automa work on iPhone?

No. Automa is for Android 8.0 or newer, on phones, tablets, foldables and Chromebooks.

Where can I download Automa?

From the Automa page: tap Download Automa 1.9.9 (APK), open the file on an Android phone and allow Install unknown apps if asked. Automa has also been submitted to APKPure, and will be released there once APKPure finishes its review.
