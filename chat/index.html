CorX Chat is an AI workspace that runs on your own ChatGPT account. You connect once with the official Sign in with ChatGPT browser extension, and from then on you can talk to the top models your plan can reach, switch between them mid-conversation, send images, and get complete working code back — collected in a canvas you can download.

It behaves as an agent by default. Tell it to write code, fix a bug, plan a build or work through a long multi-step job and it does the whole thing — there is no mode to switch on first.

What it does

A chat window that does the work.

Agent by default

Ask it to build something and it builds it — full files, worked through to the end, with its reasoning available in a dropdown on every answer. No toggle, no "shall I continue?".

Every model your plan has

The picker is populated live from your own account, so it lists exactly what you can reach. Switch mid-conversation and keep the thread.

A real terminal

Full CPython in your tab. It writes code, runs it, reads the real traceback when it breaks and fixes it — installing packages, reading and writing files, and reaching the internet. You watch every command, and can close the panel any time.

Canvas & downloads

Every code block it writes is collected in the side panel with its filename, and downloads in one click.

Upload anything

Images are read directly. Every other file is staged into the sandbox so the agent can open it, edit it and hand back a new version.

Your language, your region

It answers in the language you write in, and your locale is passed along so place-dependent answers are relevant to where you are.

Your account, your keys

Credentials are handled by the official extension and stay encrypted in your browser. CorX Labs never receives or stores them.

Being straight about the edges. The sandbox is a Python VM, not a Linux box — there is no git, no node, no apt, and packages are limited to what micropip can install. Image generation is not wired in yet, and there is no ranked search index behind the internet access, so give it a URL when you can. Everything else above runs against your real account today, and nothing on this page is a mockup.

Questions

Before you connect.

What is CorX Chat?

An AI workspace from CorX Labs that runs on your own ChatGPT account. It gives you the top models your plan can reach behind one interface, with image input, a downloadable code canvas, and an assistant that behaves as an agent without needing a mode switched on.

Do I need an account?

Not a CorX one. You sign in with your existing ChatGPT account through the free “Sign in with ChatGPT” browser extension. Your credentials stay encrypted in your browser — CorX Labs never sees or stores them.

Why is a browser extension needed?

The OAuth flow for this client only permits redirects the extension can handle, so a hosted website cannot complete sign-in on its own. Press Connect and CorX Chat will tell you whether the extension is needed and link you straight to it.

Do I have to turn on agent mode?

No. Just tell it what you want built. It writes complete code, works through multi-step jobs to the end, and never asks you to enable something first.

Is it free?

The interface is free. Model access comes from your own ChatGPT account, so which models you can reach and what limits apply are governed by your existing plan, not by CorX Labs.
